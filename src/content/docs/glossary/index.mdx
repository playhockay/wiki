A living reference of Hockay terminology. Add entries by opening a
[pull request](https://github.com/playhockay/wiki)—the "Edit this page" link
below lands on GitHub's web editor for this file. Cross-link generously—most
terms have a dedicated page.

## A

- **Advisory**—see [LPCHUIP advisories](/modifiers/advisories).
- **All-Star games**—two exhibition matchdays after [The Final](/guides/season-cycle#the-final), one per conference, played by the Verglas All-Stars and the Givre All-Stars. Excluded from standings and odds. Exists because a [petition](/guides/petitions) carried.
- **The Almighty Ice**—the god of Hockay. Alive, intent-ful, spreading. [→ Page](/lore/almighty-ice)
- **Allegiance vote**—the ballot on [Stewardship](/lore/stewardship), one to each register. Two thirds of the frozen register to align. Forty-six declined. Busan and Helsinki did not. [→ Page](/lore/stewardship)
- **Ancient**—ambiguous player modifier. Also the [Atlantis](/teams/atlantis-amonites) arena modifier, whose effect is [Glacified](/lore/glacified). [→ Page](/modifiers/player#ancient)
- **A.P.S.P.**—l'Agence des Pétitions et Sceaux Publics. Receives filings, issues seals. [→ Page](/guides/petitions)
- **Arena modifier**—semi-permanent trait tied to a team's home building. One per arena, forty-eight in total. [→ Page](/modifiers/arena)
- **Assessment**—what a citizen owes in [wealth tax](/guides/taxes). What the balance cannot cover stands *outstanding* until S.L.U.S.H. settles it on Tuesday.
- **Aurora Tuesdays**—[calendar rule](/guides/calendar-rules). Player modifiers amplified.
- **Awake**—positive player modifier. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#awake)

## B

- **Binder**—where a citizen's [cards](/guides/cards) live. One hundred cards. A [sociétaire](/guides/la-caisse) holds a second.
- **Blister**—a sealed packet of [cards](/guides/cards), bought from La Confrérie with Looneys and nothing else. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **Le Blister des Séries**—the first special edition, S03P. Every postseason team of Season 3, Rare or better, three a day. Never struck again. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **The Blue Glaze**—a [Miracle](/guides/church#miracles). The lights fail and the ice produces its own.
- **Bracket**—the playoff structure. Each conference runs its own—Wildcard, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, Conference Final—and the two winners meet in [The Final](/guides/season-cycle#the-final). [→ Page](/guides/season-cycle#playoffs)
- **Brittle**—negative player modifier. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#brittle)
- **B.U.T.**—Bets Underwriting Team. Underwrites all bets and ensures payout. Grades a bet at the horn and pays it at the close of the matchday. [→ Page](/lore/organizations#butbets-underwriting-team)
- **Buyback**—Graine Wetsky Funds' standing bid for citizens' kcups. The only standing bid a Ⓚ has. [→ Page](/guides/ice-shavings)

## C

- **La Caisse**—La Caisse Populaire des Fanatiques Partisans du Hockay. Le Council's member-owned fund, paid in real dollars. It does not sell civic voice. [→ Page](/guides/la-caisse)
- **Calendar rule**—a league-wide rule attached to a day of the week. [→ Page](/guides/calendar-rules)
- **C.A.R.D.**—Confrérie Artistique pour la Reproduction et la Distribution, signed *La Confrérie*. Prints [cards](/guides/cards). Does not appraise them. [→ Page](/lore/organizations#cardla-confrérie)
- **Card**—a player in a season, or a single game, struck by La Confrérie and numbered. It does not update. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **The Church of the Ice**—forty-eight parishes, one to each arena. Petitioned into existence. [→ Page](/guides/church)
- **Clean Ice**—a [match modifier](/modifiers/match#clean-ice) declaring no modifier. Announced as a modifier.
- **Condition**—a [card](/guides/cards)'s wear, from Minted down to Scrapped. Every trip through the Recycler costs one step.
- **Conference**—[La Conférence du Verglas](/lore/conferences#verglas) or [du Givre](/lore/conferences#givre). Twenty-four teams each. [→ Page](/lore/conferences)
- **Conference Final**—the last round inside a conference. Best of three. The winner goes to [The Final](/guides/season-cycle#the-final).
- **La Confrérie**—see **C.A.R.D.**
- **Consecration**—giving [Ice Shavings](/guides/ice-shavings) to a parish reliquary. Final. Nothing comes back.
- **Consultation**—a ballot Le Council puts to the community. [→ Page](/guides/petitions#consultations)
- **Counter**—a different half put up against an offer on [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele). Only the citizen who filed the offer may accept one.

## D

- **Daily Register**—the checklist at `/today` of what a citizen can still do today. Resets at midnight UITC.
- **Donation**—a one-way gift of shares or Looneys, recorded on the public roll. A gift and not a sale. [→ Page](/guides/donations)
- **Dormancy tax**—a tenth of the Looneys of any citizen who has not claimed a daily reward in seven days. [→ Page](/guides/taxes)
- **Double or Nothing**—[match modifier](/modifiers/match#double-or-nothing). Double points, double payouts.
- **Doubled**—ambiguous player modifier. One event per game duplicates. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#doubled)

## E

- **Echo**—positive player modifier. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#echo)
- **Edition**—a special print with its own runs and its own numbers, starting at one. The first is **S03P**. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **Emolument**—§7 of the [Stewardship Offer](/lore/stewardship). A daily distribution to holders, "at a rate the Funds determine and may revise".
- **The Exchange**—the stock market in the forty-eight teams, settled by S.L.U.S.H. [→ Page](/guides/exchange)

## F

- **The Final**—the last playoff game. One-hour periods. Season 3's is played on neutral ice, at [Graine Wetsky Arena](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena). [→ Page](/guides/season-cycle#the-final)
- **Finish**—Standard, Frost, Gold or Foil. Separate from rarity. A Common in Foil is not a mistake. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **Fiscal Observation Office**—the office of Le Council that declined to read aloud how far Looneys grew in the first week of October. [→ Page](/lore/the-jambono)
- **Fist-Fight Fridays**—[calendar rule](/guides/calendar-rules). Fights and penalty minutes roughly double.
- **Flicker**—arena modifier at [The Watch Station](/teams/anchorage-auroras).
- **Fog**—[match modifier](/modifiers/match#fog). Goalies see less.
- **Foggy**—negative player modifier. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#foggy)
- **Frozen register**—the electorate of an [allegiance vote](/lore/stewardship), fixed when the ballot opens. Shares bought afterwards count for nothing. Shares sold afterwards still count.

## G

- **Givre**—the pale ice: frost and rime, soft-surfaced, feathered. One of the two conferences. [→ Page](/lore/conferences#givre)
- **la Glacière**—where O.F.F.R.E. holds both halves of an open offer. A share in it still earns its dividend and keeps its vote. [→ Page](/guides/marche-gele)
- **Glacified**—the Hockay term for classified. [→ Page](/lore/glacified)
- **The Glaze Settled**—the [Miracle](/guides/church#miracles) that leaves a [Mark](/guides/church#the-mark). The light leaves the ice and stays on one player.
- **G.L.A.S.S.**—Graphical Live Audiovisual Streaming Service. The community-funded live rink renderer. Exists because a [petition](/guides/petitions) carried.
- **Graine Wetsky Arena**—the forty-ninth building. Recovered ice, a jurisdiction of its own, no article in its name. "The Ice did not raise this one." [→ Page](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena)
- **Graine Wetsky Funds**—the institution behind [Series H](/lore/series-h), [Series I](/lore/series-i), the Recovery Assemblies, and an arena. Origins Glacified. [→ Page](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds)

## H

- **H.A.B.S.**—Hockay Accessibility and Beginner Subvention. Funds the enrolment bonus.
- **Hall of Fame**—where honoured players, teams and fans are inducted, with the date. [→ Page](/guides/honours)
- **Hollow**—weird player modifier. Effects [Glacified](/lore/glacified). [→ Page](/modifiers/player#hollow)
- **H.O.R.N.**—Hockay Official Relay Network. The broadcast arm of LPCHUIP. [→ Page](/lore/organizations#hornhockay-official-relay-network)
- **Hockay**—the sport. Pronounced "Hockay" or "Hoqué." Not "Hockey."
- **Hokcay**—the spelling in Graine Wetsky Funds' own filings. Not a typo. Not to be corrected.

## I

- **Ice Shavings**—the second currency, denominated in kcups (Ⓚ). What The Jambono leaves behind. [→ Page](/guides/ice-shavings)
- **Inter**—the label on a cross-conference matchup. No conference tag, no accent.
- **Intersaison**—the gap between seasons. The S.H.I.F.T., the Resurfacing, the dividends, and sometimes twenty-four new teams. [→ Page](/guides/season-cycle#the-intersaison)
- **Isolation**—arena modifier at [The Last Terminal](/teams/vladivostok-vodkas). Suppresses all external modifiers on visitors.

## J

- **The Jambono**—resurfaces reality. Caretaker or corruptor. [→ Page](/lore/the-jambono)
- **Jambono-Cooked**—the distinction issued to every citizen reached by The Jambono's second pass in October. Not an honour. Not a penalty. A record. [→ Page](/lore/the-jambono)
- **JM**—Jean-Michel "JM" Laflèche. Play-by-play commentator. [→ Page](/lore/commentators)

## K

- **kcup (Ⓚ)**—the unit of [Ice Shavings](/guides/ice-shavings). Exactly one Hockay Puck of volume.

## L

- **The Last Mile**—[match modifier](/modifiers/match#the-last-mile) on the final day of the regular season. Standings points count twice. Payouts do not.
- **Listed**—the mark on a card in a binder that is held in an open offer on [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele). It cannot be recycled.
- **Looneys (Ⱡ)**—the official currency of Hockay. Cannot be bought. [→ Page](/guides/economy)
- **LPCHUIP**—Le Permanent Council du Hockay and Unexplained Ice Phenomena. The governing body. [→ Page](/lore/lpchuip)
- **L.U.R.E.**—League Unit for Recruitment and Enrolment. The referral programme. "It issues bonds. It does not vouch for whom you bring."
- **Lure**—arena modifier at [The Still Strait](/teams/stockholm-sirens).
- **Luminous**—positive player modifier. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#luminous)

## M

- **Magnetic**—ambiguous player modifier. [The Puck](/lore/the-puck) finds them. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#magnetic)
- **Le Marché Gelé**—where citizens trade directly with one another: Looneys, shavings, shares, cards. Administered by O.F.F.R.E. [→ Page](/guides/marche-gele)
- **The Mark**—what a player carries after The Glaze Settled. Enveloped, Wrapped, Touched, Blanched, or Frostbitten. [→ Page](/guides/church#the-mark)
- **Match modifier**—per-game condition announced in an advisory. [→ Page](/modifiers/match)
- **Matchday**—a full slate of 24 games, one at the top of every hour. Season 3's regular season has 46.
- **Mercury**—[match modifier](/modifiers/match#mercury). Losing team gets cumulative boost.
- **Miracle**—something The Ice does at a parish, unannounced and unexplained. [→ Page](/guides/church#miracles)
- **Muted Mondays**—[calendar rule](/guides/calendar-rules). Arena modifiers suppressed league-wide.

## N

- **Net worth**—Looneys in hand, plus Looneys committed elsewhere, plus every share at what S.L.U.S.H. would settle today. Since 20 August. [→ Page](/guides/taxes)
- **Notice of Abandonment**—a private letter to a citizen who has not claimed a daily reward in thirty-five days. Seven days to appear, or the shares return to the pool. [→ Page](/guides/exchange)

## O

- **O.D.D.S.**—Office of Data-Driven Speculation. Publishes odds. [→ Page](/lore/organizations#oddsoffice-of-data-driven-speculation)
- **O.F.F.R.E.**—Office for the Fair Facilitation of Reciprocal Endowment. Administers [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele). Le Council does not. [→ Page](/lore/organizations#offreoffice-for-the-fair-facilitation-of-reciprocal-endowment)
- **O.M.E.N.**—Oracle for Mathematical Estimation and Numerology. NOT affiliated. [→ Page](/lore/organizations#omenoracle-for-mathematical-estimation-and-numerology)
- **Open ice**—the part of a team's register still sitting on the pool, unbought. Drives the brokerage rate. [→ Page](/guides/exchange#open-ice)
- **Open Ice Levy**—the brokerage band that rises as a register empties. A share leg on Le Marché Gelé pays it too, where it exceeds the commission. [→ Page](/guides/exchange#the-brokerage)
- **Outbid**—a [S.H.I.F.T.](/guides/shift) bid that stood and lost the contest. Shareholders do not choose between bids. They can only reject them.
- **Overclock**—arena modifier at [The Neon Crossing](/teams/tokyo-titans). More events per period.

## P

- **Parish**—the congregation at an arena. A parish is not a building; the building was already there. [→ Page](/guides/church)
- **Period**—15 minutes in regular season, 60 minutes in [The Final](/guides/season-cycle#the-final).
- **Petition**—a formal filing to l'Agence. On reaching threshold, Le Council must act. [→ Page](/guides/petitions)
- **Player modifier**—trait gained, lost, or swapped in [the Sixth](/lore/the-sixth). [→ Page](/modifiers/player)
- **Print number**—a card's serial within its run, assigned in the order it was struck. There is only ever one number one.
- **The Puck**—the referee. Speaks once per season, enciphered. [→ Page](/lore/the-puck)

## R

- **Rarity**—Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic or Legendary. Set by the player, not by the packet. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **Reblister**—five cards drawn blind from everything ever recycled. The same cards, not copies. Opens at Ⱡ1,000. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **Recovery Assembly**—the Funds' machine in every building that catches shavings before the ice takes them back. Rebuilt deeper twice in September. [→ Page](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds)
- **Recovery Zone**—the jurisdiction constituted on 3 September whose territory is [Graine Wetsky Arena](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena), and nothing else.
- **Recycling**—handing a card back to La Confrérie for Looneys. The card goes into the bin, one condition worse. [→ Page](/guides/cards)
- **The Register**—Le Council's record of [Miracles](/guides/church#miracles), kept since 16 August.
- **Regulation sheet**—1,012,800 kcups of Ice Shavings, per R.I.N.K. [→ Page](/lore/organizations#rinkthe-royal-institute-for-nascent-kinetics)
- **Reliquary**—where consecrated [Ice Shavings](/guides/ice-shavings) go. It does not give things back. [→ Page](/guides/church#the-reliquary)
- **Rest Day**—the day after [The Final](/guides/season-cycle#the-final). No games. No announcements.
- **The Resurfacing**—what [the Jambono](/lore/the-jambono) does between seasons. Since 7 October, also the ledger entry left on every citizen reached by its second pass. [→ Page](/lore/the-jambono)
- **Returned**—a [rivalry](/guides/rivalries) held in both directions.
- **R.I.N.K.**—Royal Institute for Nascent Kinetics. Affiliated institution. Nature [Glacified](/lore/glacified). [→ Page](/lore/organizations#rinkthe-royal-institute-for-nascent-kinetics)
- **Rivalry**—a one-way grudge, named by fans or earned in a postseason elimination. One earned in the postseason does not fade. [→ Page](/guides/rivalries)
- **Riviera**—arena modifier at [The Coastal Pavilion](/teams/rimini-rinklers). The only dual modifier.
- **The Roll**—the roll of parishes, the roll of [donations](/guides/donations), the roll of [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele), or the roll of [sociétaires](/guides/la-caisse). Le Council keeps all of them and interprets none.
- **Run Deep**—B.U.T.'s playoff market. One per qualifying team, one rung per round. A team's tickets lock while its series is on.

## S

- **Second Chance Sundays**—[calendar rule](/guides/calendar-rules). The only one that changes a result directly.
- **The Second Reckoning**—the Season 2 awards ballot. Thirteen categories, twelve leaders, one tie. [→ Page](/guides/honours)
- **Series H**—the round that opened team equity to fans. [→ Page](/lore/series-h)
- **Series I**—the round that took every register from one million shares to five. [→ Page](/lore/series-i)
- **Sharp**—positive player modifier. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#sharp)
- **S.H.I.F.T.**—Seasonal Hearing on Interclub Formations and Transfers. The transfer window. [→ Page](/guides/shift)
- **The Sixth**—the sixth dimension. Where the penalty box leads. [→ Page](/lore/the-sixth)
- **Sixth Saturdays**—[calendar rule](/guides/calendar-rules). Returns from the Sixth shift to an even gain/lose/swap.
- **S.L.U.S.H.**—Société for the Liquidation of Unsettled Share Holdings. Settles the Exchange, and on Tuesdays, unpaid tax. [→ Page](/guides/exchange)
- **Sociétaire**—a member-owner of [La Caisse](/guides/la-caisse). Not a shareholder. Owed no return.
- **S.T.A.M.P.S.**—Seals of Trust Awarded to Meritorious Public Submissions. One per citizen per administrative day, and you must choose what to spend it on—unless you are a [sociétaire](/guides/la-caisse), in which case parish and petition stop competing for it. [→ Page](/guides/petitions#on-the-seal)
- **Standing instruction**—the order in which S.L.U.S.H. sells a citizen's shares to cover an unpaid assessment. The dearest first, unless told otherwise. [→ Page](/guides/taxes)
- **Standings markets**—season-long bets on a team finishing first in its conference, or making the playoffs.
- **Star rating**—public 1–5 summary of a player's ability. The stars are a guide. The Ice is the authority.
- **Steam**—arena modifier at [The Dark Sauna](/teams/helsinki-howlers).
- **Stewardship**—the alignment of a team's register with Graine Wetsky Funds, on the terms of the Stewardship Offer of 30 September. Busan and Helsinki are Stewarded. [→ Page](/lore/stewardship)
- **Syrup**—arena modifier at [The Oldest Rink](/teams/montreal-maples).

## T

- **T.A.P.E.**—Team Alignment & Patronage Evaluation. Le Council's psycho-athletic profiling instrument for team selection.
- **Thankless Thursdays**—[calendar rule](/guides/calendar-rules). Backup goalies start.
- **Thin Ice**—[match modifier](/modifiers/match#thin-ice). Skating reduced for everyone.
- **Tidal**—weird player modifier. Effects [Glacified](/lore/glacified). [→ Page](/modifiers/player#tidal)
- **T.R.A.D.E.**—Tribunal for the Registration and Adjudication of Displaced Employees. Convenes the S.H.I.F.T. [→ Page](/guides/shift)
- **Translated**—weird player modifier. Language [Glacified](/lore/glacified). [→ Page](/modifiers/player#translated)
- **Tribute**—the ceremonial levy on a [donation](/guides/donations). Paid by the giver. Burned.

## U

- **UITC**—Universal Ice Time Coordinated. Aligns with UTC by "non-coincidence." [→ Page](/guides/faq#what-does-uitc-stand-for-again)
- **U.I.P.I.**—Unexplained Ice Phenomena Index. Le Council's classification scale, revised up and down over seasons.
- **Unexplained Ice Phenomenon**—Le Council's preferred term for anything it cannot explain. [→ Page](/lore/lpchuip)

## V

- **Verglas**—the clear ice: hard, glassy, continuous, without grain. One of the two conferences. [→ Page](/lore/conferences#verglas)

## W

- **Wandering**—negative player modifier. [→ Page](/modifiers/player#wandering)
- **Wealth tax**—a banded levy, assessed every matchday, on citizens holding more than a hundredth of everything in Hockay. Most never will. [→ Page](/guides/taxes)
- **Weird Wednesdays**—[calendar rule](/guides/calendar-rules). The home side plays under the visitor's arena condition.
- **Whiteout**—arena modifier at [The Frozen Dock](/teams/busan-blizzards).
- **Wildcard**—a single game for the eighth seat. The eighth seed hosts a visitor drawn from ninth through twenty-fourth. [→ Page](/guides/season-cycle#playoffs)
- **Wishes**—arena modifier at [The Sandy Parlor](/teams/dakar-djinns).
- **Written off**—whatever Looneys and shares together could not raise for an assessment. Not carried, not recorded, not pursued. [→ Page](/guides/taxes)
