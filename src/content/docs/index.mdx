## Contribute

Anyone can edit. Click **Edit this page** at the bottom of any article to open
the `.mdx` source in GitHub's web editor—sign in with GitHub, edit, and your
change becomes a pull request. For bigger edits, [clone the repo](https://github.com/playhockay/wiki)
and run it locally.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/playhockay/wiki/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for the house rules.
