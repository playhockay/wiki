## The arena—lore

There should not be wind inside The Howling Harbour. It is a roofed building. There is wind inside The Howling Harbour. The Almighty Ice appeared on the waterfront and brought the harbor's weather in with it, and the Whales—who grew up being shoved around by the air—learned faster than anyone to stop resisting it. A pass that leaves a visiting stick straight will arrive curved. The home team accounts for this without seeming to think about it. [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) installed instruments to measure the indoor gale and the instruments blew away. The Whales play a heavy, physical, gleeful game, as if the chaos is a teammate. It is, more or less.

## The arena—building

A low, dark, salt-bitten structure that hunkers against the harbor like it expects to be hit. The exterior is weathered black timber and grey steel, streaked with brine that no one cleans because it returns by morning. Inside, the roof is high and ribbed like an overturned hull, and a constant draft moves through it from no identified source. The seating is close and steep, the crowd loud in a flat, wind-cut way. Ropes and cleats decorate the concourse, functional or decorative depending on who you ask. The Almighty Ice surface ripples faintly at the edges in a way ice does not. Flags hang from the rafters and none of them hang still.
