<Infobox variant="ice" title="How this list came to be">
[The Almighty Ice](/lore/almighty-ice) appeared simultaneously in twenty cities. Players were already in jerseys. The rules were already known. In **Season 2** it appeared in four more. Over the Season 2 [intersaison](/guides/season-cycle) it appeared in twenty-four more, and the league went to forty-eight.
</Infobox>

Forty-eight teams, 15 players each, split into two [conferences](/lore/conferences) of
twenty-four. A team plays a round-robin inside its own conference—every rival home and
away—followed by the [playoffs](/guides/season-cycle#playoffs). Cross-conference games are
labelled **Inter**.

Teams are marked *S2* or *S3* by the season they arrived. The unmarked twenty founded the
league.

## How to pick a team

You do not pick a team. You open this page, you look at the names, the cities, the colors,
and something will feel correct. That feeling is not random. [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip)
cannot explain it further. Go with it.

Your team is also your [parish](/guides/church). Change your team and your parish changes
with it. Le Council does not recommend this and will not be recording an opinion on those
who do it often.

## Rosters

15 players per team—three forward lines, two defence pairs, two goalies. Additional players
exist as free agents. Each carries a public [1–5 star rating](/guides/faq#what-are-star-ratings).
Players evolve over time through [modifiers](/modifiers/) gained from gameplay events and time
in [the Sixth](/lore/the-sixth), and move between teams at the [S.H.I.F.T.](/guides/shift).

## Not teams

Two squads appear on the schedule and in no standings table: the **Verglas All-Stars** and the
**Givre All-Stars**, who play two exhibition matchdays after the Final. They are gated out of
standings, odds, and every team's record.

## Expansion

[The Almighty Ice](/lore/almighty-ice) appeared in twenty cities, then four more, then
twenty-four more in a single interseason during which Le Council revised the map so many times
that the map began to feel less like a record and more like a suggestion. If The Ice decides to
appear elsewhere again, there will be more teams. Le Council does not control this.

Le Council notes that forty-eight is a number that divides. Le Council has declined to say
whether this is a coincidence.
