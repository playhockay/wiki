## The arena—lore

The Pyramid Basin sits low, as if pressed into the ground by something heavier than a building. The Almighty Ice appeared here last among the [Season 2](/lore/almighty-ice) cities and behaved as though it had always intended to. The Crocodiles are the most patient team in the league; they will trail for two periods and then end a game in ninety seconds, and the crowd will not have moved the entire time. [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) requested the arena's age and received the answer "yes." Visiting goaltenders report a recurring sense that the water under The Almighty Ice is deeper than the floor allows. There is no water. There is no floor below the floor. The sense persists.

## The arena—building

A vast, low structure of sand-colored stone, broad and flat, with a single great basin sunk into its center where The Almighty Ice lies. The exterior carries no signage—the league gave up commissioning some. Inside, the architecture is monumental and unhurried: wide stone tiers, deep shadows along the upper rows, and lighting that never quite reaches the corners. Sound travels strangely; a whistle at one end arrives at the other a half-beat late. The boards are dark, near-black, and warm to the touch despite the surface. The crowd is enormous and almost silent until it is not. When the Crocodiles strike, the Basin makes a noise that registers as old.
