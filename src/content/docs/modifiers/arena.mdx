---
title: Arena modifiers
description: Semi-permanent traits tied to team locations. One per arena, forty-eight in total.
---

Every team's home arena has a modifier. Unlike [match modifiers](/modifiers/match), arena
modifiers are always active at that venue, known in advance, and part of team identity.
Unlike [player modifiers](/modifiers/player), they don't travel—an arena modifier only
applies when the team is playing at home.

There are forty-eight, one to each arena. The original twenty debuted with the league; the
four marked **S2** arrived with the [Season 2](/lore/almighty-ice) expansion, and the
twenty-four marked **S3** arrived with the [conference split](/lore/conferences).

<Infobox variant="neutral" title="One day a week they do not apply">
On **Muted Mondays** every arena modifier in the league is suppressed and there is no home-ice
boost. On **Weird Wednesdays** the home side plays under the *visitor's* condition instead of
its own. See [calendar rules](/guides/calendar-rules).
</Infobox>

## Notable cases

- **Riviera** (Rimini) is the only **dual** arena modifier. One of two sub-effects—*Dolce Vita* or *Sunburn*—is revealed at game start. [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) has failed to predict which will appear.
- **Hybrid** (Cyprus) is the only modifier that is *another* modifier. The Chimeras are assigned a random arena condition before each game, never the same one twice in a row. Le Council has stopped trying to track this.
- **Isolation** (Vladivostok) suppresses *all* external modifiers on visiting players—including ones gained from [The Sixth](/lore/the-sixth). You arrive at The Terminal stripped bare.
- **Depth** (Oman) goes further and suspends every player modifier on *both* sides for a full period. The Gulf strips everything. You are alone in the water.
- **Ancient** (Atlantis) applies a modifier whose effect is [Glacified](/lore/glacified). Le Council has filed forty-seven inquiries. None have been answered. The modifier applies regardless.
- **House Rules** (Monaco) is the only arena modifier that touches [the betting book](/guides/economy) rather than the ice.
- **Penguins** (McMurdo) is the only modifier whose enforcement mechanism is, officially, a group of spectators. The penguins are watching. They are disappointed in you.
- **Wishes** (Dakar) may reverse *any* outcome—a miss becomes a goal, a save becomes a miss. Be careful what you wish for.
