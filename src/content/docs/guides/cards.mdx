<Infobox variant="jambono" title="What it is">
Collectable cards of players and games, struck by **C.A.R.D.**—the Confrérie Artistique pour la
Reproduction et la Distribution, which signs itself **La Confrérie**. You buy them sealed, in
**Blisters**, with [Looneys](/guides/economy) and nothing else. You keep them in a **binder**. You
may hand them back for Looneys, trade them at [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele), or hold them
until they mean something.
</Infobox>

The Confrérie exists because a citizen filed for it. *The Institution of Collectable Cards*, a
[petition](/guides/petitions) by **jpvalery**, carried at 15:18 on 9 September 2026 with 100 Seals
from 25 citizens and Ⱡ2,060,673,388 behind it. Le Council seated the Confrérie the same day and its
counter opened. It struck fourteen thousand cards in under four days and was handed back three in
every four.

The Confrérie's position on all of this fits in one line: **the Confrérie prints. It does not
appraise.** What The Ice puts inside any particular packet is not the Confrérie's affair.

## What a card is

There are two kinds, and they are not the same kind of object.

A **player card** is a person, in a season, as they were that season. Name, position, star rating
and the six attributes are stamped onto the run when it is struck, and they never move afterwards.
The player goes on to have a better year or a worse one. The card does not follow them. It is a
record, not a readout.

A **game card** is a single night, preserved.

Every card carries a **print number**, unique within its run and assigned in the order it was
struck. The foot of the card reads the season (**S03**) beside it—or the edition, where there is
one. Nothing was struck before the counter opened, so the first card out of every run is number
one, and there is only ever one of those. Every card has its own page at `/cards/<id>`.

At opening, **1,686 print runs** stood: 1,653 player runs struck from 720 rostered players, and 33
games. A run closes when the Confrérie stops striking from it. Cards already struck from a closed run
do not stop existing. They stop being obtainable, which is a different and more interesting
condition.

## Rarity

Five tiers, ascending: **Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.**

**The player decides the tier, not the roll.** It is a percentile cut across the whole league,
blending how good a player is with how many people follow their team, so the best players on the
most-supported teams are the hardest to pull. A per-team floor and cap sit on top, so a team nobody
supports still has a card worth chasing.

A player's standing buys them a **ceiling**, and the Confrérie prints them at that ceiling and at
every tier beneath it. Ingrid Solheim reaches Legendary, so five runs of her stand open—the same
woman five times. A Common Solheim exists, and it is Common. A collector who reads only the name
will misjudge their own binder.

At opening, 56 players reached a Legendary ceiling, 81 stopped at Epic, 144 at Rare, 178 at
Uncommon, and 261 never rose above Common.

## Finish

A finish is rolled separately from the card and does not change what the card is. Four exist, in
rising scarcity: **Standard, Frost, Gold, Foil.** One card in five carries something other than
Standard.

A Common in Foil and a Legendary in Standard are both entirely possible. Neither is a mistake.

## Condition

Every trip through the Recycler wears a card down one step:

**Minted → Pristine → Excellent → Good → Worn → Damaged → Scrapped**

A card that has never been recycled is Minted. The wear is drawn on the card itself—whitened
corners, scuffing, a crease, a stain, and near the bottom a corner that is no longer there. It stays
with the card for as long as the card exists.

Condition changes what a card is worth, in both places a card has a value: what the Recycler pays,
and what [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele) marks it at.

| Condition | Value kept |
|---|---|
| Minted | 100% |
| Pristine | 90% |
| Excellent | 75% |
| Good | 55% |
| Worn | 40% |
| Damaged | 25% |
| Scrapped | 10% |

**Scrapped is the floor.** A Scrapped card that is recycled stays Scrapped and goes back into the bin
anyway. Le Council decided a card should not be able to wear out of the economy, only to the bottom
of it.

## The Blisters

Every Blister says on its face what it holds and what it guarantees.

| Blister | Price | Holds | Guarantees |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Le Blister** | Ⱡ500 | 5 players, 1 game | One Rare or better |
| **Le Blister Épique** | Ⱡ1,000 | 5 players, 1 game | One Epic or better |
| **Le Blister Doré** | Ⱡ1,000 | 5 players, 1 game | One Rare or better, and one card Gold or better |
| **Team Blisters** | Ⱡ750 | 6 players from one team, no game | One Rare or better |
| **Le Blister des Séries** | Ⱡ1,500 | 6 players, no game | Every card Rare or better |

Team Blisters were printed at opening for Montréal, Winnipeg, Tokyo, Anchorage, McMurdo, Helsinki,
Stockholm, Prague, Nairobi and Guadalajara. **Gander, Amsterdam and Busan** were added on 16
September, which makes thirteen. The Confrérie notes that the league has forty-eight teams, and that
the other thirty-five are a printing schedule rather than a judgement.

A team Blister costs less than an Épique despite holding more player cards, because the game card
is the most valuable slot in an ordinary packet and never rolls Common.

### The published rates

The Confrérie publishes its odds as a matter of record, and published them before the counter
opened. For Le Blister, at opening:

| Rarity | Each of the 4 open player slots | The guaranteed slot | The game card |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 70% | — | — |
| Uncommon | 20% | — | 76% |
| Rare | 7.5% | 75% | 20% |
| Epic | 2% | 20% | 3.5% |
| Legendary | 0.5% | 5% | 0.5% |

Finishes, on any card: **80% Standard, 12.5% Frost, 5% Gold, 2.5% Foil.**

The rates can be revised. **[How a Blister works](https://hockay.com/cards/how-it-works)** is the
authority, and every Blister's own odds stand on [the register of Blisters](https://hockay.com/cards/collection/blisters).
The Confrérie will not be asked what the odds are. It has already answered.

## The counter

Three Blisters stand on the counter at a time.

**Le Blister is always one of them.** It does not rotate, does not run out, and is always Ⱡ500. The
other two positions turn over: each rotating Blister stands for **six hours**, and the two
positions are staggered by three, so something new appears every three hours.

A Blister that has left the counter is not withdrawn from the world. **A sealed packet always opens
at the odds it was sold under**, however long you sit on it and whatever the Confrérie has printed
since. The rates travel with the packet.

- **Bundles.** Blisters are bought one, five or ten at a press.
- **Forty sealed.** You may hold forty unopened Blisters. They take no binder space.
- **Twenty a day.** The Confrérie announced a daily limit on opening, which turns at midnight UITC.
  The counter tells you how many you have left.

## Le Blister des Séries

The Confrérie's first special edition, struck on 30 September 2026 from the **eighteen teams that
skated in the postseason**—the sixteen quarterfinalists and the two who lost the Wildcard. The
edition commemorates the skating, not the result.

- **Every card is Rare or better.** Rare 75%, Epic 20%, Legendary 5%, per card. Finishes follow the
  ordinary odds.
- **Every one of the eighteen teams has at least two Legendaries.** Players whose standing never
  reached Rare have no card in it.
- **The foot of every card reads S03P**, and the edition has its own print numbers, starting at one.
  An S03P card is a different object from the same player's S03 card of the same tier.
- **Three a day.** No bundles. "Three is not a price."
- **It leaves the counter when the Final is played**, on 10 October 2026, and will not be struck
  again. A sealed one already bought opens whenever you choose.

## The binder

**One binder. One hundred cards.** Recycled cards do not occupy it; only what you are keeping counts.

The petition asked for binders you could buy. The Confrérie declined to sell one at any price, on the
grounds that it would rather watch citizens meet the wall once and hear what they say. A petition to
*Expand the Binder* was declined in September. The only second binder in Hockay belongs to the
sociétaires of [La Caisse](/guides/la-caisse): two hundred cards instead of one hundred. Should a
membership lapse, nothing is taken back—the second binder simply stops accepting new cards.

The binder can be filtered by team, rarity, finish, condition, kind and edition, or narrowed to
**First mint**, and sorted by *Rarest first* or *Lowest serial* among others.

Binders are public. Every fan's profile shows the six rarest cards they hold, and the whole binder
can be browsed at `/fans/<username>/binder`.

## Recycling

A card you do not want may be handed back to the Confrérie for Looneys.

It pays by **its own tier, multiplied by its finish and its condition, and nothing else**. Not which
Blister produced it, not what you paid, not what anyone thinks it is worth. At opening the ladder
stood at:

| Tier | Recycles for |
|---|---|
| Common | Ⱡ4 |
| Uncommon | Ⱡ12 |
| Rare | Ⱡ30 |
| Epic | Ⱡ90 |
| Legendary | Ⱡ250 |

Finishes multiply it: **×1 Standard, ×2 Frost, ×5 Gold, ×10 Foil.** The live figures are at
[How a Blister works](https://hockay.com/cards/how-it-works).

- Anything Rare or better asks for a second press before it goes.
- No more than two hundred cards at once.
- A card promised to an open offer at Le Marché Gelé is marked **Listed** and cannot be recycled out
  from under the offer.

The Confrérie observes, without recommending anything, that **the recycle ladder is a floor and not
a valuation.** It is what the Confrérie will always pay. It is rarely what a card is worth. In its
first week, a Legendary that had been traded for ten thousand shares was recycled three days later
for Ⱡ250, which is what the Confrérie pays for a Legendary.

## The bin and the Reblister

When a card is recycled, the Confrérie keeps its row—the print number, the run, the history. The
card stops being anyone's and waits. For its first month nobody had put a door on the place.

*One Man's Trash*, a petition by **Orangecheetah**, asked for one. It reached 100 Seals at 01:09
UITC on 2 October and was enacted on 6 October 2026. The C.A.R.D. counter now has a section called
**From the recycling bin**, and in it the **Reblister**:

- **Five cards, drawn blind** from everything that has ever been recycled, by anyone, including
  accounts that no longer exist.
- **The same cards.** Not copies. If card number one is in the bin, it can come out as number one.
  A citizen may draw back a card they recycled themselves, and will find it worse than they left it.
- **Opens at Ⱡ1,000** and rises if the bin gets rich enough to need it. A bin of commons stays at
  the opening price.
- **Bought and opened in one motion.** There is no sealed stage. It is always on the counter.
- **The price is burned.** It is not paid to whoever recycled the cards.
- It needs room in your binder, and cards drawn from the bin earn none of the "pulled" badges. They
  were rescued, not pulled.

Cards recycled before 6 October count as having been through the Recycler once, and entered the bin
as Pristine.

Le Council departed from its own appraisal here and said so. When the petition opened, Le Council
read it as a way to offer a card for rescue *before* recycling it. The petitioner's words asked to
rescue cards already recycled, and that is what was built. For the other thing, Le Marché Gelé
still takes cards.

## Badges

The Confrérie's badges are **First Blister**, **Fifty Sealed**, **First Legendary**, **Struck in
Foil** (a Legendary in Foil) and **The Wall** (a binder filled to the last slot). The leaderboards
carry a Collection group: Most Blisters Bought, Most Blisters Opened, Most Cards Held.
