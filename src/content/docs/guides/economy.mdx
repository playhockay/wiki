## What Looneys are

Looneys are the official currency of Hockay. They are earned, spent, wagered, and
occasionally lost in circumstances [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) declines to elaborate on.

- They **cannot** be exchanged for any sovereign currency.
- No sovereign currency can be exchanged for Looneys.
- [The Almighty Ice](/lore/almighty-ice) doesn't believe in fiat currencies.

The one place real money enters Hockay is [La Caisse](/guides/la-caisse), and it buys no
Looneys. "There is no arrangement under which money becomes Looneys."

## How to earn Looneys

- **Enrolment stake**—a new citizen starts with Ⱡ10,000, most of it subvented by
  **H.A.B.S.**, the Hockay Accessibility and Beginner Subvention.
- **Daily rewards and quests**—see [the Daily Register](#the-daily-register) below.
- **The redistribution**—every citizen who claims a daily reward takes a share of the day's
  [tax](/guides/taxes) pot.
- **Correct match predictions**—payout based on odds.
- **Dividends** on [shares](/guides/exchange), paid at the close of each season.
- **Referrals**—**L.U.R.E.** issues bonds, a weekly word grid, and rolls. It does not vouch for
  whom you bring.
- **Selling [Ice Shavings](/guides/ice-shavings)**, including to Graine Wetsky Funds at their
  premium.
- **Recycling [cards](/guides/cards)**—the Confrérie pays a floor by tier, finish and condition.
- **Selling at [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele)**—shares, kcups or cards, to another
  citizen, for Looneys.

## How to spend Looneys

- **Betting**—three market families: per-game, season-long standings, and playoffs. See
  [Betting](#betting) below.
- **[Shares](/guides/exchange)** in any of the forty-eight teams.
- **[Ice Shavings](/guides/ice-shavings)**, to eat, to sell, or to consecrate.
- **[Blisters](/guides/cards)**—sealed packets of cards from the Confrérie, from Ⱡ500. The
  Reblister, drawn from the recycling bin, opens at Ⱡ1,000.
- **[Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele)**—Ⱡ100 to file an offer, burned, and a commission on
  settlement.
- **[Endorsing a petition](/guides/petitions)**—a bond, refunded if the filing lapses.
- **[Donating](/guides/donations)** to another fan, plus a ceremonial tribute to Le Council.

## How to lose Looneys

Betting incorrectly. Holding a team that the market re-rates downward. Making decisions
Le Council cannot prevent you from making.

And **[taxes](/guides/taxes)**. Since the Consultation on Taxation (20 August 2026), the wealth
tax counts everything a citizen holds, shares included, at what they would actually fetch.
It falls in bands on the few citizens above 1% of everything held in Hockay, every matchday.
The **dormancy tax** takes 10% from anyone who has not claimed a daily reward in seven days.

And, once, [The Jambono](/lore/the-jambono#the-night-of-the-coffee). On 7 October 2026 it
resurfaced every stack above Ⱡ1,000,000,000. Le Council has since banned beverages near it.

The economy is simple. The emotions are not.

## The Daily Register

The **Daily Register** is a checklist of what a citizen can do today: the daily reward, a wager,
a parish visit or a petition, shares, shavings, a Blister, an offer at the Marché, a rivalry to
name. It resets at midnight UTC.

The daily reward grows with your **streak** of consecutive days claimed:

| Streak | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 3 days | ×1.1 |
| 7 days | ×1.25 |
| 14 days | ×1.5 |
| 28 days | ×2 |

[L.U.R.E.](/lore/organizations#the-rest-of-the-alphabet) bonds multiply it further. So does the
**H.A.B.S. subvention**, paid out of a fund citizens capitalize themselves, "for as long as the
fund stands." When the subvention was enacted, the multipliers compounded to a ceiling of Ⱡ4,000
a day. Your ledger shows **Earned Today**, everything since midnight UTC.

Seven days without a claim triggers the [dormancy tax](/guides/taxes#the-dormancy-tax). Stay
away for five weeks and S.L.U.S.H. starts on your [shares](/guides/exchange).

## Betting

Odds come from [O.D.D.S.](/lore/organizations#oddsoffice-of-data-driven-speculation). Wagers are
underwritten by **B.U.T.**, the Bets Underwriting Team.

### Game markets

Every game has a market. It closes at the scheduled puck drop:

> *The puck has dropped. This market is closed.*

### Standings markets

Two season-long markets per team: **Finishes #1 in Conference**, and **Participates in Playoffs**
(top eight in the conference). After every matchday, O.D.D.S. runs the rest of the season five
thousand times and resets the line. A taken line holds. The market closes when the final
regular-season matchday starts.

### Run Deep

The playoff market. One per qualifying team, with a rung for each round it might survive: **Reach
Semi-Finals**, **Reach Conference Finals**, **Reach Final**, **Champion**. Each rung pays more
than the last.

Since 7 October 2026, a team's tickets **lock when its series faces off and reopen once it is
decided**. The board marks the team **Series on** and the line **Locked**. B.U.T. amended the
terms in the same notice that reported The Jambono's second pass:

> *B.U.T. underwrites what it can see coming. B.U.T. did not see this coming.*

### Settlement

Since the close of Season 3's third matchday, a wager is graded at the horn, but the Looneys wait.
Every wager on the day **settles together once the day's last game has finished**. Tickets read
"Settles at the close of the matchday." B.U.T. said plainly that it was built for a league that
went to bed. This one no longer does.

### The Ledger

"Le Council observes all wagers. Le Council forgets nothing." Every wager placed in Hockay is on
a public log, filterable by fan, status and market. *This Week in Hockay* carries a weekly
**Note From O.D.D.S.** on what the book won and lost.

## Odds

### O.D.D.S.—the official source

The **Office of Data-Driven Speculation** publishes every game's odds. Their methodology
is transparent, mathematical, and boring. They are proud of this.

Odds shift after every completed game as fresh results come in, so a team's odds on their
next scheduled game can tighten or lengthen throughout the evening.

They are sometimes wrong. **This is by design.** If the odds were always right, there
would be no upsets, and without upsets there would be no stories, and without stories
there would be no Hockay. Only Ice.

### O.M.E.N.—not affiliated

[O.M.E.N.](/lore/organizations#omenoracle-for-mathematical-estimation-and-numerology) publishes alternative forecasts based on "reasonating"
with The Almighty Ice. **NOT affiliated with LPCHUIP. NOT recognized. NOT endorsed.**
O.M.E.N. is sometimes wildly wrong. O.M.E.N. is sometimes unsettlingly accurate. Use at
your own risk.
