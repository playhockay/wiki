<Infobox variant="ice" title="What it is">
A parish is not a building. The building was already there.
</Infobox>

There are forty-eight parishes, one to each [arena](/teams/). You belong to the one at your
team's rink. There is no separate enrolment, no register to sign and nothing to choose—
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) took the view that a citizen who has already declared an allegiance
should not be made to declare it a second time in a different hand.

Change your team and your parish changes with it. Le Council does not recommend this and will
not be recording an opinion on those who do it often.

## How it came to exist

The Church was not created by Le Council. It was **petitioned into existence**.

*The Founding of the Church of The Almighty Ice* was filed on 14 July 2026 by the citizen
**ManLee92** and carried twelve days later, at 02:42 UTC on the twenty-sixth. Le Council
reviewed it for procedural grounds to decline and found none. It has recorded that it looked
twice.

The arithmetic is worth setting down, because Le Council found it slightly unsettling:

- **Sixty-six Seals were required. Sixty-six Seals were affixed.** Not sixty-seven. The roll
  closed on the exact figure [l'Agence](/guides/petitions) had set.
- **Ⱡ999,999 were required. Ⱡ1,000,000 were contributed.** Funded to precisely one Looney
  beyond threshold. No single citizen appears to have arranged this.
- **Nineteen citizens** carried it, across twelve days, at one Seal apiece per day—very nearly
  the maximum the mathematics permits. The nineteen did not miss many mornings.

This is the first [petition](/guides/petitions) Le Council ever enacted.

## Attendance

**Attendance is a STAMP, presented once a day.**

It is the same STAMP l'Agence issues for the endorsement of petitions. There is one. It is
issued daily, it does not accumulate, it cannot be bought at any price and it cannot be
recovered once pressed.

<Infobox variant="sixth" title="The choice">
A citizen who spends the day's Seal at the parish has not spent it on the roll of filings. A
citizen who spends it on a filing has not been counted at the parish. Le Council has been asked
whether this arrangement is deliberate. Le Council has acknowledged the question.
</Infobox>

Serve the bureaucracy or serve the god. Every day. That is the whole mechanic.

## The reliquary

The reliquary receives [Ice Shavings](/guides/ice-shavings).

Shavings offered are **given, not sold**. No Looneys change hands, no entry is raised against
any ledger of exchange, and nothing is returned. What [The Jambono](/lore/the-jambono) leaves
behind between periods may be eaten, may be sold to any party willing to buy it—and there is at
present a party willing to buy it—or may be consecrated.

Consecration is final. The shavings do not go into a vault; they go into the parish, and the
parish does not give things back. Le Council advises citizens to be certain before they are
generous.

Shavings are deliberately a poor substitute for turning up. The rate is punitive and Le Council
has revised it upward at least once. Money is not meant to outrank attendance, and where it has
looked like it might, the rate has moved.

## The Roll

Le Council publishes the roll: every parish, its lifetime attendance and its lifetime relics.
The figures only ever climb.

> *"Le Council keeps the roll below as a matter of record and offers no guidance on how a parish
> might rise."*

Asked what the roll is *for*, Le Council forwarded the question to the only party in a position
to answer it, and notes—as it has before, and expects to again—that no reply has been received,
and that this is not unusual.

## Miracles

<Infobox variant="neutral" title="Unpublished">
There is no roll of Miracles, no leaderboard, no odds and no explanation. A Miracle simply
happens in the feed. Everything in this section is community observation, not [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip)
documentation, and should be read as such.
</Infobox>

Sometimes, at a home game, something happens in the building that nobody can file calmly.
[H.O.R.N.](/lore/organizations#hornhockay-official-relay-network) relays it in one flat
sentence, records a measurement, and does not engage. What the community has pieced together:

- Only the **home** parish appears to be able to draw one.
- Loud parishes draw more. The Ice appears to go where it is loudest, and a parish that stops
  showing up stops being visited fairly quickly—within about a week, on the community's count.
- Miracles get **bigger as a season goes on**, and reset when [The Jambono](/lore/the-jambono)
  resurfaces between seasons.
- They are rare. Something under one a matchday, league-wide.

### The building

Reported flatly, never explained, never once acknowledged as strange by anyone on the call.
Nothing on the ice changes.

| Miracle | What H.O.R.N. relayed |
|---|---|
| **The Ice Vibrated** | The surface held a note. Le Council has the recording and has elected not to release it. |
| **The Lights Flickered** | The lights failed in sequence and recovered. The sequence has been noted. |
| **The Net Levitated** | The net left the ice and returned. Play was not stopped. |
| **The Puck Froze** | The Puck stopped in the air and then continued. Its trajectory was unchanged. |
| **The Boards Rang** | The boards sounded. Nobody struck them. |
| **The Building Was Still** | For one second nothing in the building moved. Le Council does not include the players in this description. |

### The eruption

The same six things, harder, at parishes that have been turning up for a while.

| Miracle | What H.O.R.N. relayed |
|---|---|
| **The Shockwave** | The Puck was struck and the building moved with it. A structural review was scheduled and has since been cancelled. |
| **The Blue Gaze** | The lights failed. The ice produced its own light and held it. Le Council was present and has no statement. |
| **The Boards Bowed** | The boards flexed outward, held, and returned to true. The figures are within tolerance for a material that does not do this. |
| **The Cold Came Up** | The temperature fell and recovered. The refrigeration plant was not running at the time. |
| **The Ice Went Clear** | The surface became transparent. Le Council has been asked what was visible beneath it and has forwarded the question. |
| **The Air Went Solid** | The pressure in the bowl rose and fell. Every ear in the building has been accounted for. |

The community's working theory is that **The Blue Gaze comes first**—that a parish has to be
looked at before The Ice will do anything further there.

### The Mark

**The Gaze Settled.** *The light left the ice and remained on a single player. Le Council has
recorded the duration and the name.*

This is the only Miracle that touches a person. The player carries a Mark afterward, and the
Mark holds for as long as their parish keeps showing up. Five have been observed, and they are
not all kind:

| Mark | What is said of them |
|---|---|
| **Enveloped** | The light closed over them and did not let go. |
| **Wrapped** | The light went round them once. |
| **Touched** | The light found them. Nothing else happened. |
| **Blanched** | The colour went out of them where the light passed. |
| **Frostbitten** | The light stayed too long. |

A Mark is roughly twice the swing of anything [The Sixth](/lore/the-sixth) hands out, in either
direction. Most of the time The Ice looks at somebody and does nothing at all.

<Infobox variant="jambono" title="Not a reward">
The Ice does not take sides. A parish attends, and something happens, and it may well be
something that costs them the game.
</Infobox>

## What Le Council has said about all this

Nothing, largely. l'Agence, admitting the founding filing to the public roll, noted the legal
disclaimer attached to it and *"chose not to dwell on what its inclusion implies."* It undertook
to address the questions of institutional recognition, sainthood, and the appropriate renaming
of leaderboard categories in due course.

Le Council considers two of those three matters settled and declines to say which.
