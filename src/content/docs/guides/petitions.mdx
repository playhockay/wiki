<Infobox variant="neutral" title="Acronyms">
**A.P.S.P.**—l'Agence des Pétitions et Sceaux Publics, a subsidiary of
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip). **S.T.A.M.P.S.**—Seals of Trust Awarded to Meritorious Public
Submissions.
</Infobox>

Le Council does not, as a rule, take requests. Le Council is permanent. Le Council convenes when
Le Council convenes.

Le Council does, however, listen—and since May 2026 there has been a channel built for the
purpose. **l'Agence des Pétitions et Sceaux Publics** receives, reviews, and where deemed worthy
admits to the public roll the petitions of Hockay's citizenry.

## What a petition is

A formal request. It may concern a single team, Le Council itself, or the entirety of Hockay.
It must be plainly stated. It must be filed in good faith.

Each citizen is limited to **three petitions in flight** at a time. The cap exists to ensure
filings are considered, not produced in volume.

## The two thresholds

Every admitted petition carries two goals set by the A.P.S.P., and a date by which both must be
met:

- A goal in **Looneys**.
- A goal in **S.T.A.M.P.S.**

A petition that crosses one but not the other is, in the technical language of the A.P.S.P.,
*insufficiently sealed*. Le Council does not convene on insufficiently sealed petitions. Le
Council has been firm on this point.

Petitions that lapse return every Looney to its sender. **The Seals, having been pressed, remain
pressed.**

## On the Seal

A S.T.A.M.P.S. is issued at the rate of **one per citizen per administrative day**.

It is not a balance. It cannot be stockpiled. It cannot be bought at any price. Once pressed it
is not returned, and a new one is issued the following administrative day.

> *"Le Council notes that an endorsement which costs nothing is worth approximately that much."*

<Infobox variant="sixth" title="The daily choice">
The same Seal is what buys attendance at [your parish](/guides/church). A citizen who spends the
day's Seal at the parish has not spent it on the roll of filings. Le Council has been asked
whether this is deliberate. Le Council has acknowledged the question.

The one exception is a [sociétaire](/guides/la-caisse) of La Caisse, who may attend parish and
endorse a petition on the same day. *"Membership does not give you two Seals. It stops the two
things competing for the one you already had."*
</Infobox>

The Looneys contribution, by contrast, is a bond. Endorse a petition with Looneys and you get
them back if it lapses. If it convenes, they are considered consumed in the act of bringing the
matter before Le Council.

## What petitions have actually done

A good deal of Hockay exists because citizens filed for it and paid for it:

- **[The Church of the Ice](/guides/church).** Filed by **ManLee92** on 14 July 2026, carried
  twelve days later on sixty-six Seals and Ⱡ1,000,000 from nineteen citizens. Forty-eight
  parishes were constituted, one to each arena. It is the first petition Le Council ever
  enacted.
- **The All-Star games.** Two exhibition matchdays after the Final, played by the Verglas
  All-Stars and the Givre All-Stars.
- **G.L.A.S.S.**—the Graphical Live Audiovisual Streaming Service, the community-funded live
  rink renderer.
- **[Rivalries](/guides/rivalries).** *Rivalries that belong to the fans*, filed by **doppog** on
  30 July, carried at two minutes past one on 24 August on ninety-nine Seals and Ⱡ180,369,960
  from twenty-four citizens. Enacted that morning, with four departures, each set down.
- **[Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele).** Filed by **FatPenguin** on 31 July, carried at 23:54
  on 26 August on 180 of 180 Seals from forty citizens and Ⱡ1,008,649,661 against a hundred
  million—the first filing to pass a billion. Le Council seated a new authority,
  [O.F.F.R.E.](/lore/organizations#offreoffice-for-the-fair-facilitation-of-reciprocal-endowment),
  to run it, and departed from the petition in four places.
- **A neutral Final.** *Neutral Combined Site for Finals Game*, filed by **Assassin66** on 6
  August, carried on the 29th at 81 of 81 Seals, enacted at 00:17 UITC on 1 September. The Final
  is played on neither finalist's ice. Where it would be played stayed Glacified until
  [Graine Wetsky Funds](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds) offered [a building](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena),
  and the enacted petition left Le Council no power to decline it.
- **[Cards](/guides/cards).** *The Institution of Collectable Cards*, filed by **jpvalery** on 25
  August, carried at 15:18 on 9 September on 100 Seals from twenty-five citizens and
  Ⱡ2,060,673,388 against a billion. La Confrérie opened its counter the same day.
- **Forfeiture.** *A Petition to Begin Limiting Absentee/Zombie Encumbrance of Investment
  Tranches*, filed by **Ragnaroknrol**, carried on 11 September on 66 of 66 Seals and Ⱡ1,000,009
  against Ⱡ999,999 asked, and was enacted on the 14th. Abandoned holdings now
  [return to the pool](/guides/exchange).
- **The bin.** *One Man's Trash*, filed by **Orangecheetah** on 10 September, reached 100 of 100
  Seals at 01:09 UITC on 2 October and was enacted on 6 October. La Confrérie now keeps a bin of
  everything ever recycled, and sells it back blind. Le Council built what the petition said
  rather than what Le Council had said about it, and says so.

Not everything carries, and not everything that is filed is admitted. *Expand the Binder* was
declined.

## Consultations

Separate from petitions, and running the other direction: **consultations** are ballots Le
Council puts to the community. Le Council asks; the citizenry answers. Le Council reports the
result, not the count.

- **The Consultation on Taxation** closed at 20:00 on 20 August. It asked three things. Wealth
  means everything a citizen holds: not close. It falls in bands: carried, and the ten-largest
  rule was retired. An unpaid assessment is settled by S.L.U.S.H. selling shares: this led a
  field of three without a majority, and Le Council enacted it anyway, recording that it *"led
  rather than triumphed."* The numbers were never on the ballot. Le Council set them.
  [→ Taxes](/guides/taxes)
- **The Second Reckoning**—the Season 2 awards. Thirteen categories, one ballot each, open for
  eight days. The verdict came on 10 September: twelve categories returned a leader, and the
  thirteenth returned a tie, which Le Council declined to manufacture a winner out of.
  [→ Honours](/guides/honours)
