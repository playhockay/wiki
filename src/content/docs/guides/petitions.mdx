<Infobox variant="neutral" title="Acronyms">
**A.P.S.P.**—l'Agence des Pétitions et Sceaux Publics, a subsidiary of
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip). **S.T.A.M.P.S.**—Seals of Trust Awarded to Meritorious Public
Submissions.
</Infobox>

Le Council does not, as a rule, take requests. Le Council is permanent. Le Council convenes when
Le Council convenes.

Le Council does, however, listen—and since May 2026 there has been a channel built for the
purpose. **l'Agence des Pétitions et Sceaux Publics** receives, reviews, and where deemed worthy
admits to the public roll the petitions of Hockay's citizenry.

## What a petition is

A formal request. It may concern a single team, Le Council itself, or the entirety of Hockay.
It must be plainly stated. It must be filed in good faith.

Each citizen is limited to **three petitions in flight** at a time. The cap exists to ensure
filings are considered, not produced in volume.

## The two thresholds

Every admitted petition carries two goals set by the A.P.S.P., and a date by which both must be
met:

- A goal in **Looneys**.
- A goal in **S.T.A.M.P.S.**

A petition that crosses one but not the other is, in the technical language of the A.P.S.P.,
*insufficiently sealed*. Le Council does not convene on insufficiently sealed petitions. Le
Council has been firm on this point.

Petitions that lapse return every Looney to its sender. **The Seals, having been pressed, remain
pressed.**

## On the Seal

A S.T.A.M.P.S. is issued at the rate of **one per citizen per administrative day**.

It is not a balance. It cannot be stockpiled. It cannot be bought at any price. Once pressed it
is not returned, and a new one is issued the following administrative day.

> *"Le Council notes that an endorsement which costs nothing is worth approximately that much."*

<Infobox variant="sixth" title="The daily choice">
The same Seal is what buys attendance at [your parish](/guides/church). A citizen who spends the
day's Seal at the parish has not spent it on the roll of filings. Le Council has been asked
whether this is deliberate. Le Council has acknowledged the question.
</Infobox>

The Looneys contribution, by contrast, is a bond. Endorse a petition with Looneys and you get
them back if it lapses. If it convenes, they are considered consumed in the act of bringing the
matter before Le Council.

## What petitions have actually done

Two of the largest features in Hockay exist because citizens filed for them and paid for them:

- **[The Church of the Ice](/guides/church).** Filed by **ManLee92** on 14 July 2026, carried
  twelve days later on sixty-six Seals and Ⱡ1,000,000 from nineteen citizens. Forty-eight
  parishes were constituted, one to each arena. It is the first petition Le Council ever
  enacted.
- **The All-Star games.** Two exhibition matchdays after the Final, played by the Verglas
  All-Stars and the Givre All-Stars.
- **G.L.A.S.S.**—the Graphical Live Audiovisual Streaming Service, the community-funded live
  rink renderer.

## Consultations

Separate from petitions, and running the other direction: **consultations** are ballots Le
Council puts to the community. Le Council asks; the citizenry answers.
