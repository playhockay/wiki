<Infobox variant="ice" title="The terms">
A donation is a gift and not a sale. Nothing may be asked in return.
</Infobox>

A citizen may bestow **shares** or **[Looneys](/guides/economy)** upon another as an act of
loyalty, fellowship, or an inexplicable desire to put all of one's eggs into a single basket.
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) levies a ceremonial tribute on the giver and records every donation
on the public register.

Le Council calls the register **The Roll of Donations**. It keeps it in perpetuity.

## It is not a trade

A donation never touches a team's pool. It moves no price, prints nothing on the chart, and
takes no open ice. It only moves a position—or a balance—from one account to another.

This matters for two reasons. Buying a bloc through donations is **cheaper than buying one
through the market**, which moves the price against you the whole way up. And shares carry
voting weight at the [S.H.I.F.T.](/guides/shift), so supporters who pool their holdings may come
to control a team's front office.

Le Council permits this. Le Council notes only that the register is public, and that many hands
make light work.

## The tribute

The tribute is paid by the **giver**, on top of the gift, and is burned. It is not arbitrary—
each rate is anchored to what the same manoeuvre would have cost through ordinary channels:

| Donation | Tribute | Why that number |
|---|---|---|
| **Shares** | 4% of the block's market value | A round trip through the market costs 2% to sell plus 2% to buy back. Donating is never the *cheaper* way to move a position—you are paying for the absence of slippage, not dodging the brokerage. |
| **Looneys** | 10% | The daily wealth-tax rate. Parking Looneys in a friend's account to duck the levy should cost roughly what the levy costs. |

Le Council tunes both rates live. The rate shown on the form is the rate that applies.

## The gates

Le Council has fenced the practice rather than forbidding it:

- **A lock period.** What arrives cannot be passed onward for a while, and donated shares cannot
  be sold during it either.
- **Rolling caps** on value moved, applied in **both** directions—on what you send and on what
  you receive.
- **A minimum**, below which nothing is recorded. Dust does not go on the roll.
- **A minimum account age** before an account may send at all.
- **The 33% cap.** S.L.U.S.H. declines a donation that would take the recipient past a third of
  a team that is not theirs, on exactly the same terms it declines the purchase. See
  [one team to a citizen](/guides/exchange#one-team-to-a-citizen).

You cannot endow yourself. The Ice notices these things.

## The register

Every donation is entered publicly: who gave, who received, what moved, the tribute burned, and
when. Messages attached to a donation are published with it.

Le Council offers no commentary on its contents. When the roll is empty it says only:

> *Nothing has been given yet. The penguins are watching.*
