<Infobox variant="jambono" title="Currency">
**Ice Shavings**, denominated in **kcups** (**Ⓚ**). One Ⓚ is exactly one Hockay Puck of volume.
</Infobox>

[The Jambono](/lore/the-jambono) resurfaces the ice between every period. The shavings it leaves
behind have been in contact with [The Almighty Ice](/lore/almighty-ice) itself.

They accumulate as each game plays on. **The unsold supply melts twenty-four hours after the
final horn.**

## Where they come from

Every period end mints a batch. When the marketplace opened in June, a batch was 25 to 70
kcups. Every increase since has come from the **Recovery Assembly** of
[Graine Wetsky Funds](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds), which catches the shavings at the boards
before the ice takes them back:

| From | Per period end | Off the top to the Funds |
|---|---|---|
| 15 June | 25–70 Ⓚ | — |
| 29 July | 250–700 Ⓚ | 10% |
| 12 September | 1,000–2,000 Ⓚ | 20% |
| 20 September | 2,000–4,000 Ⓚ | one third |

[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) negotiated the first. **Le Council was not asked** about the other two.
It learned of the 12 September rebuild from a notice the Funds addressed to the team registers,
and of the 20 September one the day before, by which time the units were in transit.

The case for July was that three things were draining the boards: the slate went from ten games a
matchday to twenty-four, [the Church's reliquary](/guides/church#the-reliquary) destroys
everything offered to it, and the Funds take their cut before the marketplace sees any of it. At
the old rate, the three of them together would have left the market dry.

The market has cleared every night since June anyway, at every depth. **There is still no
surplus at the horn.** The price of a kcup does not move with volume.

The postseason thins the slate from twenty-four games a night to a handful, and the recovery
falls with it—by roughly nine tenths from 30 September.

## What you pay

Price per kcup moves with two things:

- **A standings premium.** Shavings from a game involving a top-of-the-table team cost more.
  Shavings off the bottom of the standings are cheap.
- **A supporter's discount.** If your team played in the match, you pay less.

Graine Wetsky Funds also run a **buyback desk**, purchasing kcups back from fans at a premium
over the marketplace price. It is the only standing bid a kcup has. It is not the highest price a
kcup has fetched: in the week the buyback was quoting Ⱡ369, two deals at
[Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele) settled at Ⱡ4,000 a kcup, and from 17 September four
settled at Ⱡ10,000.

What the Funds wanted with them was [Glacified](/lore/glacified) until 28 September. Their own
answer, lodged that morning: *"Shavings Make Arenas."* See
[Graine Wetsky Arena](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena).

## What they are for

Three things, and you must pick one:

1. **Eat them.** You spend the Looneys. You eat the shavings. The Almighty Ice is aware that you
   have done this. No flavor has been confirmed. No flavor has been denied.
2. **Sell them**—to the Funds at their standing bid, or to another citizen at
   [Le Marché Gelé](/guides/marche-gele), at whatever the two of you agree.
3. **[Consecrate them](/guides/church#the-reliquary)** as relics at your parish. Given, not sold.
   No Looneys change hands and nothing is returned. Consecration is final: the shavings do not go
   into a vault, they go into the parish, and the parish does not give things back.

The relic rate is deliberately punitive. Shavings are purchasable and Seals are not, so a great
many consecrated shavings are needed to equal one person turning up. Money is not meant to
outrank attendance.

Consecration is the only true sink in the economy. On 28 September Le Council reported that the
parishes had destroyed more shavings that season than any institution in Hockay had ever
assembled.
