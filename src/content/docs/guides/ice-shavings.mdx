<Infobox variant="jambono" title="Currency">
**Ice Shavings**, denominated in **kcups** (**Ⓚ**). One Ⓚ is exactly one Hockay Puck of volume.
</Infobox>

[The Jambono](/lore/the-jambono) resurfaces the ice between every period. The shavings it leaves
behind have been in contact with [The Almighty Ice](/lore/almighty-ice) itself.

They accumulate as each game plays on. **The unsold supply melts twenty-four hours after the
final horn.**

## Where they come from

Every period end mints a batch. Both the mint rate and the price are tuned live by
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip), and the rate has been raised twice—once for Season 3, then again by
an order of magnitude. Three things pushed it up: the slate went from ten games a matchday to
twenty-four, [the Church's reliquary](/guides/church#the-reliquary) destroys everything offered
to it, and [Graine Wetsky Funds](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds) take a cut off the top before the
marketplace sees any of it.

At the old rate, the three of them together would have left the market dry.

## What you pay

Price per kcup moves with two things:

- **A standings premium.** Shavings from a game involving a top-of-the-table team cost more.
  Shavings off the bottom of the standings are cheap.
- **A supporter's discount.** If your team played in the match, you pay less.

Graine Wetsky Funds also run a **buyback desk**, purchasing kcups back from fans at a premium
over the market price. What the Funds want with them is [Glacified](/lore/glacified).

## What they are for

Three things, and you must pick one:

1. **Eat them.** You spend the Looneys. You eat the shavings. The Almighty Ice is aware that you
   have done this. No flavor has been confirmed. No flavor has been denied.
2. **Sell them**—to another citizen, or to the Funds at their premium.
3. **[Consecrate them](/guides/church#the-reliquary)** as relics at your parish. Given, not sold.
   No Looneys change hands and nothing is returned. Consecration is final: the shavings do not go
   into a vault, they go into the parish, and the parish does not give things back.

The relic rate is deliberately punitive. Shavings are purchasable and Seals are not, so a great
many consecrated shavings are needed to equal one person turning up. Money is not meant to
outrank attendance.
