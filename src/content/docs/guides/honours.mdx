<Infobox variant="ice" title="How it works">
After a season closes, Le Council opens a ballot—one per category, every category final once submitted. The count stays [Glacified](/lore/glacified). Le Council publishes the verdict, never the arithmetic. Winners are inducted into the **Hall of Fame**.
</Infobox>

## The Hall of Fame

> *"The Ice remembers. Le Council records. These are the ones who left a mark."*

The Hall of Fame on hockay.com lists every inductee under **Players**, **Teams** or **Fans**,
with the honour and the date of induction. It can also be sorted by newest or oldest.

## Season 2—the Second Reckoning

Opened on **31 August 2026**, with Season 3 already well under way, and closed on 8 September. Le
Council noted that a reckoning does not expire, and that it has no mechanism by which one could.
The verdict was published on **10 September**, and the class inducted the same day.

**Thirteen categories.** Seven returned from the Inaugural Honours, along with Most Devoted Fan.
Five were new, because Season 2 produced things Season 1 had no need to name. **Best Podcast
Host** and **Best Commentator** were retired. Le Council offered no explanation.

| Category | Winner |
|---|---|
| Most Valuable Player | Valentina Ospina (Medellín Mapaches) |
| Best Goalie | Freya Magnusdottir (Anchorage Auroras) |
| Best Forward | Valentina Ospina (Medellín Mapaches) |
| Best Defender | Ingrid Solheim (McMurdo Monoliths) |
| The Enforcer | Sakura Shimizu (Tokyo Titans) |
| Sniper of the Season | Sakura Shimizu (Tokyo Titans) |
| Iron of the Sixth | Dmitri Volkov (Montréal Maples) |
| Newcomer of the Season *(new)* | Valentina Ospina (Medellín Mapaches) |
| Team of the Season *(new)* | [Medellín Mapaches](/teams/medellin-mapaches) |
| Climb of the Season *(new)* | [Guadalajara Gatos](/teams/guadalajara-gatos) |
| Petition of the Season *(new)* | *The Founding of the Church of The Almighty Ice*, filed by ManLee92 |
| Market Mogul *(new)* | FatPenguin |
| Most Devoted Fan | **doppog** and **Tiinyhandz**, tied |

Twelve categories returned a leader. The thirteenth returned an exact tie, and Le Council
declined to manufacture a winner out of it. Both citizens are named Most Devoted Fan for Season 2.

Some things the verdict put on the record:

- **Valentina Ospina** took three honours on one ballot. Le Council noted the correlation with
  Medellín's Team of the Season and offered no theory for it.
- **Sakura Shimizu** won The Enforcer and Sniper of the Season, and tied for second in Iron of
  the Sixth. Roughly two ballots in three named her Sniper.
- **Dmitri Volkov** is the only player to hold the same honour in both seasons: Iron of the Sixth.
- Best Goalie was among the closest categories Le Council has certified on any ballot—*"the width
  of a shift."*
- The [Church](/guides/church) founding was the only outright majority among the three petitions
  named.
