**Graine Wetsky Funds** is the financial institution behind
[the Exchange](/guides/exchange). It arrived with a term sheet already signed.
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) read the document twice, found no trap, and signed.

Its origins are [Glacified](/lore/glacified).

## What it owns and operates

- **S.L.U.S.H.**—the Société for the Liquidation of Unsettled Share Holdings, a joint venture
  between Le Council and the Funds, which settles every trade on the Exchange.
- **A position in the market itself.** The Funds take stakes in teams as they see fit. Each stake
  is published, set apart from the fans as an *Institutional* row, on the team's **Ownership** tab
  and its Exchange page. Since 1 October the Funds also vote those stakes (see
  [Stewardship](/lore/stewardship#the-funds-vote)).
- **A share off the top of every recovery.** Before the [Ice Shavings](/guides/ice-shavings)
  from each period reach the marketplace, the Funds take their share. It is not fixed: ten
  percent to 11 September, twenty percent from 12 September, one third from 20 September. The
  share is published. The Funds' running total sits on their fan page, where anybody may read it.
- **A shavings buyback desk**, a standing bid for kcups from fans.
- **GWF Abyssal Logistics**, which owns and operates the *Sounding*—the only vessel rated to
  descend to [The Sunken Hall](/teams/atlantis-amonites). Le Council raised the matter of the
  depth rating. Le Council was told the depth rating is current.
- **The Recovery Assembly**, in all forty-eight buildings. It began as a trough along the dasher
  boards; since 12 September it takes the full perimeter. Financed, owned and maintained by the
  Funds, at what Le Council reported at the time was no cost to anyone. Le Council has since
  withdrawn that characterization.
- **[Graine Wetsky Arena](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena)**, the forty-ninth building, in a
  jurisdiction whose territory is the arena.

## The Recovery Assembly

Installers call it *the skirt*. It catches what [The Jambono](/lore/the-jambono) leaves at the
boards before the ice takes it back. Le Council granted it on 22 July. Every rebuild since was
announced rather than asked for.

| From | Per period end | Funds' share | How Le Council learned of it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 June (marketplace opens) | 25–70 Ⓚ | — | — |
| 29 July | 250–700 Ⓚ | 10% | Negotiated. The Funds opened higher; Le Council declined twice. |
| 12 September | 1,000–2,000 Ⓚ | 20% | A notice to all forty-eight registers at 06:00 UITC, addressed to somebody else. |
| 20 September | 2,000–4,000 Ⓚ | one third | Notified on the 19th, by which time the units were in transit. |

Each rebuild happened overnight, in every building, with no building closed and no game moved.
**Nothing was added.** Le Council's assessors report the same machine, reaching further under the
same sheet: *"The first Assembly arrived at the heap before the sheet did. This one arrives before
the heap does."*

Le Council noted the form of the third figure, having no comment on its size: the first two were
percentages, and this one is a fraction of the whole. It also noted that each of its advisories on
the subject was shorter than the last.

The marketplace has cleared every night since June. **There is still no surplus at the horn.**

## One regulation sheet

On 16 September [R.I.N.K.](/lore/organizations#rinkthe-royal-institute-for-nascent-kinetics)
published a figure nobody had asked for:

> **One regulation sheet of ice is 1,012,800 kcups of Ice Shavings.**

The working: a Puck displaces 115.825 cm³. A sheet is 61.0 × 30.0 metres at 25 mm, or 45.75 m³,
or 394,992 kcups if the kcups were solid. Settled shavings carry 0.390 of the density of the sheet
that produced them. 394,992 ÷ 0.390 = 1,012,800. *"The p-value is 1. R.I.N.K. considers this
satisfactory."* R.I.N.K. closed by noting that it had no reason to have calculated this.

**At 18:13 UITC on 27 September, the Funds' holding passed it**—at La Ladera, in the eighteenth
of twenty-four games on matchday 44, at the resurfacing after
[Medellín](/teams/medellin-mapaches) beat [Phuket](/teams/phuket-podcasters) 3-2 in a shootout.
Two matchdays and fifty-four games were still to play. *"Whatever this was built to be sufficient
for, it became sufficient early."*

Seventeen hours later, the Funds lodged [Graine Wetsky Arena](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena) with all
forty-eight registers. Its sheet is 1,012,800 kcups exactly.

## The periodic disclosure

On 26 September the Funds lodged a periodic disclosure with every register, on schedule and in
the prescribed form. Le Council carried it in full and found it complete. Its expenditure for the
period:

| Item | Ⱡ |
|---|---|
| Recovery Assembly, second generation—forty-eight installations | 41,826,000,000 |
| Recovery Assembly, third generation—forty-eight installations | 65,479,000,000 |
| Maintenance, all buildings | 3,184,400,000 |
| Buyback settlements to holders | 24,860,400 |
| Assets under construction, non-Hokcay jurisdiction | **418,063,000,000** |
| Professional and filing costs | 92,200,000 |

The fifth line is **the largest single figure ever filed with a Hockay register**: nearly four
times both Assembly generations combined, nearly seven times the combined reserve of all
forty-eight teams on the Exchange, and more than thirteen times every Looney held by every
citizen. It did not appear on the previous filing.

Le Council does not know what it is. "Non-Hokcay jurisdiction" is a permitted description. It
describes everywhere that is not here.

The filing's notes include one line Le Council did not remark on: *"Shavings recovered under this
arrangement remain our property until deployed."*

## Le Council Has Refused Nothing

On 29 September Le Council published the whole record of its dealings with the Funds since July:

| Date | Matter | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| 22 July | Recovery Assembly; ten percent off the top; a standing buyback | **Granted** |
| 6 September | A statement entered into the record beside the ruling on the venue of the Final | **Granted** |
| 12 September | Assembly, second generation; share to twenty percent | *Not sought* |
| 20 September | Assembly, third generation; share to one third | *Not sought* |
| 26 September | Periodic disclosure, Ⱡ418,063,000,000 under construction elsewhere | *Not required* |
| 28 September | Graine Wetsky Arena | *Not sought* |

*"The Funds stopped asking because there had ceased to be a question."*

Le Council found no breach, and would grant both requests again. It concluded that it has
authority over the record and none over the buildings, and that the Assemblies are in the
buildings: **"Le Council's no costs nobody anything."** Its answer was to ask for a fund of its
own—[La Caisse](/guides/la-caisse) Populaire des Fanatiques Partisans du Hockay, which opened
the same day. *"A
shareholder is owed a return and a sociétaire is not."*

The second offer came on 30 September, with a condition, and the registers voted on it. Two

## How the Funds write

Every filing is warm, exclamatory, and correct. Le Council has checked every factual claim in
every one of them and has found nothing to correct, and has said it would have preferred to.

The filings spell the sport *Hokcay*, call the teams *clubs*, and capitalize their Values—Proud,
Fair, Ambition, Stewardship. Le Council reproduces them exactly, and beneath the notices of 12
and 28 September added the same line: *"The forty-eight are teams."*

The Funds' statements so far:

> More ice for more people is the Ambition. Kindness costs nothing. Excellence costs a little
> more, and we are happy to pay it. *(12 September)*

> **Shavings Make Arenas.** It has been our Ambition from the first day and we have kept it in
> plain sight the whole way, because we have nothing to hide and never did. *(28 September)*

## What Le Council has learned about the paperwork

Le Council signed Series H having read it twice. It read it a third time the morning
[Series I](/lore/series-i) was served, in a way it should have read it before signing, and
reports the following:

> **The agreement contains no requirement to consult.** Not on issuance, not on quantum, not on
> timing. The Funds were not obliged to tell Le Council, and did not.

Notice was served through a slot in a door that Le Council had not previously identified as a
slot.

Le Council's representative asked the Funds' representative to explain the timing. The Funds'
representative said "capital structure" three times and asked whether Le Council had any further
questions about the capital structure.

## Can they do it again

Le Council has been asked. Le Council has read Article 3.C twice more.

Article 3.C does not say how many times.

## Still unresolved

As of 7 October 2026:

- What the Ⱡ418,063,000,000 under construction is, and where.
- What the holding is for now. It passed one sheet on 27 September, the Arena's sheet was
  1,012,800 kcups, and the holding stood at 1,131,115 a week later.
- What the Funds meant by having *"more to say about parishes before the season is out."*
- Who buys the Arena's naming rights.
- What §§4 to 9 of the Stewardship schedule mean in practice. Le Council declines to say.
- Where the Funds came from.
