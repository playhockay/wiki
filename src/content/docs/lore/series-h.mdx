Le Council announced the close of Hockay's **Series H** funding round "pleased, and faintly
alarmed."

The round was led by an institution whose paperwork is impeccable and whose origins
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) elected not to examine too closely. The term sheet arrived already
signed. The terms were unusual: rather than dilute the fans, the round handed them the equity.

Le Council read the document twice, found no trap, and signed.

## What it created

- **Fan-held equity in every team.** Each team issued a fixed pool of **one million shares**,
  bought and sold with [Looneys](/guides/economy).
- **S.L.U.S.H.**—the Société for the Liquidation of Unsettled Share Holdings, a joint venture
  between Le Council and the Funds, to settle the book.
- **A brokerage fee** on every trade, which services the financing and is repaid to Le Council
  and the Funds against the terms of the round.
- **A dividend** at the close of each season, weighted by how far a team went.
- **The Funds' own position.** They take stakes in teams as they see fit, published separately
  from the fans on every team's page.

## The sentence Le Council would like back

Announcing the opening, Le Council wrote this:

> *The pools are fixed at one million for now. The current agreement sets out no terms for
> diluting or expanding them—and, Le Council notes, no terms against it either. Read into that
> what you wish.*

Le Council intended a note of caution.

Graine Wetsky Funds appears to have read an invitation. Eight weeks later it served notice under
**Article 3.C, subsection (a)(iii) of the Series H preferred terms** and issued
[Series I](/lore/series-i).
