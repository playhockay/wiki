<Infobox variant="neutral" title="One-line summary">
On 30 September 2026 [Graine Wetsky Funds](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds) asked every team to align
its register with the Funds. Each register voted. **Busan Blizzards and Helsinki Howlers
aligned. The other forty-six declined.**
</Infobox>

When the Funds gave the league [Graine Wetsky Arena](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena) for the Final on
28 September, they promised one more thing, *"the only part of this with a condition in it."*
They described the condition in advance, warmly and in public:

> We will not impose it. The clubs must choose it.

Le Council answered the offer before it arrived, by opening [La Caisse](/guides/la-caisse) (see
[Le Council Has Refused Nothing](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds#le-council-has-refused-nothing)). The
offer itself was filed with all forty-eight registers at 15:00 UITC on 30 September.

## The Stewardship Offer

The covering letter makes three promises to a register that aligns:

1. **Your holders get paid. Every day. Win or lose.** A distribution to every holder on the
   register, every day, for as long as the agreement runs. *"A dividend is a nineteenth-century
   idea."*
2. **Your team plays at the Arena.** Home fixtures at Graine Wetsky Arena at no fee, no rent and
   no share. The letter then reaches for the enacted neutral-site petition: *"a Stewarded club that
   reaches the Final will play the biggest game in the sport on its own ice."*
3. **It is permanent, and that is the point.** Alignment does not expire and does not renew. A
   register that wants out votes, and is discharged, per §9.

The letter is followed by a schedule. Le Council carried it in full, and so does this wiki:

> **Schedule of Operating Provisions**
>
> *The following provisions apply to a club whose register has recorded alignment.*
>
> **§4 · Allocation.** Recovery at a Stewarded building is operated to the Arena standard.
> Recovered volumes are allocated in full at source.
>
> **§5 · Signal.** Fixtures of a Stewarded club are relayed on the Funds' wire, which the Funds
> maintain at their own cost.
>
> **§6 · Site.** Home fixtures of a Stewarded club are staged at Graine Wetsky Arena, at the Funds'
> cost and on the Funds' calendar.
>
> **§7 · Emolument.** A supplementary distribution accrues daily to holders of record, at a rate
> the Funds determine and may revise.
>
> **§8 · Network.** Live coverage of Stewarded fixtures is carried on the Funds' network, together
> with commercial placement sold by the Funds.
>
> **§9 · Term.** Alignment, once recorded, continues in perpetuity, and is discharged upon a vote
> of the register taken in the prescribed form together with the reconstitution charge.

## What Le Council said about it

Le Council found the offer **complete, and found no misrepresentation in it.** Every undertaking
in §§1 to 3 is one the Funds are able to perform. Every provision is a plain statement of how a Stewarded team
will be operated.

**Le Council declined to summarise the schedule.** A body that summarises an instrument has taken
a position on it, and Le Council had none. It said one thing twice instead:

> **Read §§4 through 9 before you vote. Read §§4 through 9 before you vote.**

It made two observations about the drafting, and offered neither as a criticism:

- **Not one of the six provisions contains a negative.** No *not*, no *no*, no *never*, no
  *ceases*, no *shall not*—a hundred and twenty-one words without a word that describes a thing
  stopping. Le Council drafts instruments, and spent part of the afternoon trying to do the same.
- **The schedule uses one term that the schedule does not define.** It appears in no filing the
  Funds have lodged, in no advisory Le Council has published, and nowhere in the record. Le Council
  declined to say which term, and noted that a citizen who reads §§4 to 9 will find it in under a
  minute. This wiki follows Le Council's lead.

On §9, Le Council noted that it has no "prescribed form" for a discharge vote, had never had
occasion to write one, and began writing one the morning the offer was filed.

The Funds call the arrangement **Stewardship**. A team whose register has aligned is
**Stewarded**.

## The Allegiance Vote

One ballot per register, at hockay.com/allegiance, opened with the offer and closed at 00:00 UITC
on 4 October. It was put to the citizens holding shares in that register and to nobody else.

- **The register was frozen when the ballot opened.** A citizen's weight is the shares they held
  at that moment. Shares bought afterwards counted for nothing; shares sold afterwards still
  counted. A citizen who held none when the ballot opened had no ballot.
- **Two choices:** *Align the register* or *Decline*. A position, once recorded, cannot be changed
  or withdrawn.
- **The bar is two thirds**—not of the shares cast, but of every share on the frozen register.
  Le Council's reason was arithmetic: at a simple majority, sixteen of the forty-eight registers
  are decided by one citizen acting alone. At two thirds, two are.
- **Nobody is aligned by failing to appear.** Weight that never arrives counts, in effect, against
  the offer. This is the inverse of [the S.H.I.F.T.](/guides/shift), where silence is consent. Le
  Council chose the inversion deliberately: a transfer is ordinary business. This is not.
- **The roll is public.** Every position, and its weight, is listed on the ballot's page.

The ballot was not about the Final. The Final is at Graine Wetsky Arena either way.

### The Funds vote

On 7 August, in *The Interseason Begins*, Le Council had written:

> **Graine Wetsky Funds does not vote.** It holds as much as a quarter of some teams and has never
> expressed a preference about anything.

**At 12:00 UITC on 1 October, Graine Wetsky Funds voted**—to align, in all thirty-one registers
in which it held a share, at the weight it held when each ballot opened.

Le Council corrected its own figure the same day. The Funds' largest position was
[Winnipeg](/teams/winnipeg-wendigos), at 10.1 percent, then [Dublin](/teams/dublin-dragons) at
1.4, [Montréal](/teams/montreal-maples) at 1.3 and [Medellín](/teams/medellin-mapaches) at 1.2,
and nothing above one percent anywhere else. In seventeen registers the Funds held nothing.

They voted [Guadalajara](/teams/guadalajara-gatos) on **one share** of 4,809,560. In thirty of the
thirty-one, the position cast was under one and a half percent of the register. Le Council drew
the only conclusion it said the arithmetic supported: *"the object was not the outcome. It was the
roll."*

**A share is a share.** Le Council has no instrument that stops a holder voting one, and said it
would not have drafted one if asked. The sentence of 7 August was never a rule. It was a habit
Le Council had mistaken for a fact.

### The result

**Two registers aligned. Forty-six declined.**

| Register | Aligned | Of the frozen register | Bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Busan Blizzards](/teams/busan-blizzards) | 3,591,912 (74.0%) | 4,855,439 | 3,237,122 |
| [Helsinki Howlers](/teams/helsinki-howlers) | 3,598,974 (72.4%) | 4,970,624 | 3,313,916 |

The Funds' weight was not in it: 1,811 shares in Busan and 3,974 in Helsinki. In each register
one citizen held just over half and aligned them, and that was not two thirds. Each needed other
holders as well, and had them.

Across the league:

- **441 positions from 52 citizens**, alongside the Funds' thirty-one. 188 aligned; 253 declined.
- **Thirteen registers finished with more weight aligned than declined.** Eleven of them fell
  short of the bar. The closest were [Oman Orcas](/teams/oman-orcas) on 62.3 percent, short by
  193,891 shares, and [Nuuk Nanuqs](/teams/nuuk-nanuqs) on 60.2, short by 308,566.
- **[Amsterdam Aardvarks](/teams/amsterdam-aardvarks) said no loudest**: 99.7 percent of the
  register cast, 72.8 percent of it to decline.
- **46.2 percent of all shares on the frozen rolls were never cast.**

Helsinki were eliminated from the postseason on 2 October, a day before the ballots closed. Busan
were still playing when they did. Le Council noted the order and drew no conclusion from it.

## What happens now

Le Council's position, in full: *"The Schedule applies to a team whose register has recorded
alignment. Le Council has recorded two."*

What §§4 to 9 mean in practice, Le Council has declined to say. The term the schedule does not
define, the rate under §7, the prescribed form and the reconstitution charge under §9 are all
[Glacified](/lore/glacified), or not yet written.
