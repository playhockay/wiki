The Jambono resurfaces reality. It appears between periods and between seasons. Once, it went
further than the ice. Caretaker or corruptor—nobody knows. The Jambono maintains [The Almighty Ice](/lore/almighty-ice),
but it's unclear whether it works for the Ice or is trying to contain it.

Sometimes when it resurfaces, things change. Stats shift. Goals retroactively didn't happen.
A player is different.

## What it does

### Between periods (in-game)

The Jambono appears between periods of every match to clean the ice. Most of the time this
is ordinary maintenance. Occasionally, during a game, a random event is retroactively
changed after it happens. The feed updates. The community notices. Le Council has no
official position on whether this constitutes time travel.

### Between seasons (The Resurfacing)

At the end of the season, The Jambono performs **The Resurfacing**—the preparation
of the league for what comes next. Player modifiers shift. Reality is adjusted. [JM](/lore/commentators)
previews the changes. The new season begins. Things may be different.

Not to be confused with the ledger entry of the same name, below.

## The night of the coffee

In the first week of October 2026, the Looneys held across Hockay grew by a factor Le Council's
Fiscal Observation Office "declined to read aloud." Le Council convened to study the matter at
rinkside, where the ledgers are kept cold.

> *At approximately 02:40 UITC, a member of Le Council's support staff tripped on a cable that Le
> Council has since been unable to locate. A cup of coffee left the member's hand. It reached The
> Jambono.*

The Jambono did not stop. It completed its pass, then a second pass beyond the ice sheet. Le
Council's instruments recorded it crossing the boards, the press box, and the ledger. Le Council
published *The Jambono Has Resurfaced* on 7 October.

What the second pass did:

- **Every stack at or below Ⱡ1,000,000,000** was untouched—counting Looneys in hand, on open
  wagers, and owed on wagers already won.
- **Above that line, the higher the stack, the deeper the shave.**
- **Nobody passed anybody.** Every citizen who stood above another before the incident stands above
  them now.
- **Open wagers were resurfaced with their owners.** A ticket kept its odds. Its stake and its
  return were shaved by the same measure as the rest of the stack.
- **No share was taken.** Every certificate is where it was. But each team's price "followed the
  citizens who hold it": a team held by the deepest stacks fell with them, and a team held by many
  smaller hands fell least. S.L.U.S.H. recorded the new prices as found.

Each citizen reached by the second pass has a ledger entry titled **The Resurfacing**, recording
what they held before and what remains. They also hold the **Jambono-Cooked** distinction.

> *Le Council does not consider this an honour. Le Council does not consider it a penalty either.
> It is a record.*

Le Council has since prohibited beverages within four metres of The Jambono, and is aware the
measure arrives late. In the same notice, B.U.T. amended the terms of
[Run Deep](/guides/economy#run-deep).

> *Le Council does not know whether The Jambono was caretaking or corrupting. Le Council has never
> known. The coffee does not appear to have helped.*

## Where it lives

[The Stone Opera](/teams/prague-phantoms) (Prague) has a parking spot for The Jambono in
the backstage corridor. This is the only arena that publicly acknowledges accommodating
The Jambono on a regular basis, though fans at other arenas report seeing the door to the
maintenance corridor open by itself between periods.
