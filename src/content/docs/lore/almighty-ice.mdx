<Infobox variant="ice" title="One-line summary">
The Almighty Ice is the god of Hockay. Nobody started Hockay. The Almighty Ice chose.
</Infobox>

The Almighty Ice appeared one day in twenty places simultaneously, and where it appeared,
Hockay began. Nobody started the sport. The players were already there, already in jerseys,
already knowing the rules to a game that hadn't existed moments before.

It has since appeared in twenty-eight more. There are now
[forty-eight arenas](/teams/), sorted into [two conferences](/lore/conferences) by a distinction
The Ice was already making before Le Council noticed it was making one.

The Almighty Ice is alive—or at least intent-ful. It is not a surface. It is not a
substance. It is present.

## The spreading

The Almighty Ice is spreading, and it is no longer slow.

Four cities in Season 2. Then, across a single [intersaison](/guides/season-cycle#the-intersaison),
twenty-four more—in waves, with the interval between a site being declared and an arena
manifesting shrinking until it stopped being an interval at all.
[Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) revised the map so many times that the map began to feel less like a
record and more like a suggestion The Ice was choosing to ignore.

The **P.F.A.O.**—the Provisional Field Assessment Office—was deployed wherever The Ice was
converging, ahead of each new arena. All of those offices have now closed. For the first time
since spring, the forecast is empty.

The last arena did not appear on land. It was found three thousand metres beneath the North
Atlantic, and it had apparently been waiting there for the wall between the two conferences to
be built. See [the Atlantis Amonites](/teams/atlantis-amonites).

The macro-question the community should always be asking:

> *What happens when The Almighty Ice finishes spreading?*

## How it communicates

The Almighty Ice communicates through three channels, each more unsettling than the last:

1. **Through [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip)**—the preferred channel. Advisories are
   issued, conditions are named, games proceed.
2. **Directly to the ice**—arena conditions shift without notice.
   [Match modifiers](/modifiers/match) appear. The feed adapts.
3. **Silence**—the Ice goes quiet. No notifications. No observations.
   [Le Council finds the silence unusual but not unprecedented.](/modifiers/advisories)

## Appearances

- Every arena, continuously.
- As the playing surface in all 48 arenas, in one of two forms—
  [verglas or givre](/lore/conferences).
- At the [Final](/guides/season-cycle#playoffs), which has one-hour periods. The
  Almighty Ice has more time there. Strange things happen.
