Hockay is a hockey league sitting on top of a mythology that nobody in the league is willing to
call a mythology. [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) calls it a series of Unexplained Ice Phenomena and
files reports.

## The entities

- [**The Almighty Ice**](/lore/almighty-ice)—the god of Hockay. Alive, intent-ful, and still
  spreading. It appeared in twenty places at once, then in twenty-eight more.
- [**The Puck**](/lore/the-puck)—the Referee. The authority figure is the object everyone fights
  over. Speaks once a season, enciphered.
- [**The Jambono**](/lore/the-jambono)—resurfaces reality between periods and between seasons.
  Caretaker or corruptor. Nobody knows.
- [**The Sixth**](/lore/the-sixth)—the sixth dimension, behind the penalty box. Players who go
  there come back changed, or don't come back changed, which is its own kind of information.

## The world

- [**The conferences**](/lore/conferences)—verglas and givre, the two forms of ice. The Ice
  sorted the arenas. Le Council merely documented which side each was standing on.
- [**Glacified**](/lore/glacified)—the Hockay term for classified. Applies to a great deal.
- [**The commentators**](/lore/commentators)—JM calls every game and has never once acknowledged
  that anything about Hockay is unusual.

## The institutions

- [**Le Permanent Council du Hockay**](/lore/lpchuip)—LPCHUIP. Half sports league, half
  paranormal investigation unit. Always *Le Council*, never *The Council*.
- [**Related organizations**](/lore/organizations)—O.D.D.S., H.O.R.N., A.P.S.P., S.L.U.S.H.,
  T.R.A.D.E., R.I.N.K., and the unrecognised O.M.E.N.
- [**Graine Wetsky Funds**](/lore/graine-wetsky-funds)—the one institution Le Council does not
  own half of, and the only one that has ever surprised it.

## The money

- [**Series H**](/lore/series-h)—the round that handed the equity to the fans.
- [**Series I**](/lore/series-i)—the round that printed four million more shares in every team,
  through a slot in a door.

## Where to go from here

The lore is not decoration. Almost all of it is load-bearing: the entities produce
[modifiers](/modifiers/), the institutions run [the systems](/guides/getting-started#what-do-i-actually-do),
and the [petitions](/guides/petitions) fans file turn into the next thing on this list.

*Le Council reminds you that this wiki is maintained by the community. Accuracy is aspirational.
The Almighty Ice is the authority.*
